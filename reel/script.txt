Your AI agent opens one web page. And burns your whole token budget.
Raw HTML. Scripts. Menus. Ads. Thousands of tokens, just to find one button.
Meet Heron. A browser built for AI agents.
It reads the whole page as clean, compact text. A full Wikipedia article, about seven thousand tokens. Nothing cut off.
Every button and link gets a short ID. Your agent clicks by ID. No guessing selectors.
After each click, Heron sends only what changed. Not the whole page again.
It runs on Firefox, not Chromium. Plugs into Claude with M C P. And sits behind a Cloudflare gateway, with keys, quotas and usage metering.
It is honest by design. If a site shows a bot check, Heron tells you. It never bypasses it.
